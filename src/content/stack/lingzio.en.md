## Who it's for / who it's not for

**For:** Adults learning English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, or Japanese for work, travel, immigration prep, or family—who want structure plus speaking practice, and who will open the app regularly.

**Not for:** Anyone expecting to become “fluent in a month.” That slogan was **not** found as an official Lingzio promise; the closest speed claim is the brand’s **93% improved in 2 months** line, which must stay attributed to Lingzio. Also not ideal if you need a published refund/money-back page before paying (no formal refund-policy page was retrieved in research). Pure grammar textbook learners who dislike AI conversation tools may prefer another format.

## FAQ

**Can you become fluent in 30 days with Lingzio?**  
Do not expect that. Lingzio markets “learn fast / talk sooner” and claims **93% of users improved proficiency in 2 months**—a brand claim about improvement, not guaranteed fluency. Realistic short-term goals are often functional A2-style progress with daily practice, not mastery.

**What languages can I learn?**  
Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, English, and more added regularly (official FAQ).

**What is CEFR?**  
An international standard. Lingzio lessons run from A1 (beginner) to C2 (mastery).

**Do I get a certificate?**  
Yes—when you complete all lessons in a CEFR level.

**How do renewals work?**  
Monthly Plus $9.99/mo or Annual Plus $59.99/yr until cancelled; cancel anytime per FAQ copy.

**What does Lifetime Premium include?**  
Permanent Premium access, all future updates, every language, all features forever—“never expires” messaging.

**Is Lifetime a subscription?**  
No—official framing is one payment, no subscription.

**Is there a refund window?**  
A formal refund-policy page was **not** retrieved from lingzio.com in the research used for this guide. Check checkout terms before purchasing.
